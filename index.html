HOME WORKOUTS

Barbell · Kettlebell · Dumbbell · Bodyweight · Off-Day Programming
Barbell · Squat rack + hang bar · Plates (up to 45s) · KBs: 35# & 53# · DBs: 10# / 25# / 35# pairs · Med ball · Bench
How to use: These are standalone sessions — pull any one whenever you have time. Aim for 2–3 per week on days away from CrossFit. Mix categories: don't do two heavy strength days back to back. Sessions are 25–45 min. Scale loads to how you feel that day.

Strength Sessions

S1

Squat & Hinge Day

Strength Posterior Chain
~35 min barbell
Warm-Up (5 min)
  • Hip circles, leg swings, 10 air squats, 10 good mornings (empty bar)
Main Work
Back Squat — 5 × 5
Build to a moderate-heavy 5. Rest 2–3 min between sets.

Romanian Deadlift — 4 × 8
Slow eccentric (3 sec down). Moderate load.
Accessory (2 rounds)
  • 10 single-leg RDL each side — 35# KB in opposite hand
  • 15 back extensions on floor (superman holds, 2 sec pause)
  • 10 slow tempo air squats (3 sec down, pause at bottom)
The RDLs are probably the single best thing you can add at home — CrossFit rarely loads hamstrings with this kind of slow, deliberate work.
S2

Deadlift Focus

Strength
~30 min barbell
Main Work
Conventional Deadlift — 6 × 3
Build over 6 sets — last 2 should be near heavy. Rest 2–3 min.

Barbell Row — 4 × 8
Overhand grip, torso ~45°. Pull to lower chest.
Finisher (1 round)
  • Accumulate 60 sec dead hang (feet lifted, scapular active)
  • 20 hollow rocks
  • 15 push-ups
If your CrossFit programming already includes heavy deadlifts that week, drop the load here by 20% and focus on speed off the floor.
S3

Front Squat + Core

Strength Skill
~35 min barbell + KB
Main Work
Front Squat — 5 × 3
Focus on upright torso and full depth. Lighter than back squat.

Good Mornings — 3 × 10
Light load, slow and controlled.
Core Circuit — 3 rounds
  • 30 sec hollow body hold
  • 30 sec arch / superman hold
  • 10 V-ups
  • 10 KB Russian twists each side — 35# KB
Front squat position transfers directly to cleans. If your elbows tend to drop in CrossFit, drill this deliberately here.
S4

Pause Squat + Upper Pull

Strength
~30 min barbell
Main Work
Pause Back Squat — 5 × 3
3-second pause at the bottom. Much lighter than normal squat — focus on staying braced and upright, not grinding through. Rest 2 min.

Barbell Row — 4 × 10
Overhand grip. 2-second squeeze at top.
Finisher — 3 rounds
  • 10 slow tempo push-ups (3 sec down, 1 sec pause at chest)
  • 10 DB bent-over rows each arm — 35# DB
  • 10 DB bicep curls — 25# DB
Pause squats build the bottom-position strength that translates directly to catching heavy cleans. The discomfort sitting in that hole is the point.
S5

Deadlift Variation + Hip Thrust

Strength Posterior Chain
~30 min barbell
Main Work
Sumo Deadlift — 5 × 5
Wide stance, toes out, grip inside knees. Great low-stakes place to experiment if you haven't tried it. Moderate load.

Stiff-Leg Deadlift — 3 × 8
Bar stays close, minimal knee bend. Feel the hamstring stretch.
Finisher — 3 rounds
  • 12 barbell hip thrusts (upper back on bench, bar across hips, drive through heels)
  • 10 KB swings — 53# KB
Hip thrusts are one of the best glute builders available and almost never appear in CrossFit. The bench makes these much more comfortable than floor-only.
S6

Bench + KB Press Superset

Strength
~35 min barbell + DB + bench
Main Work
Bench Press — 5 × 5
Build to a moderate-heavy 5. Full ROM, controlled eccentric.

DB Incline Press — 3 × 10
35# DBs. Set bench to ~45°. Elbows at 45° from torso.
Superset — 3 rounds, minimal rest
  • 10 DB bent-over rows — 35# each arm
  • 10 DB hammer curls — 25# DBs
  • 10 tricep dips off bench
CrossFit does a lot of pressing but almost all of it is overhead or push-up based. Horizontal pressing and dedicated arm work fills a real gap here.

Conditioning Sessions

C1

Barbell Cycling EMOM

Conditioning EMOM
~25 min barbell
EMOM × 16 min — alternating:
Odd minutes: 5 hang power cleans
Even minutes: 7 barbell deadlifts

Then: 2 rounds for quality —
10 barbell bent-over rows
10 slow RDLs
Rest as needed
Loading Guide
Light / recovery day 75–95 lb
Moderate 95–115 lb
Heavy 135 lb
Keep the cleans crisp — if your elbows slow down or you're muscling it up, drop the weight. Quality reps build better movement than grinding.
C2

Bodyweight Grinder

Conditioning
~20–25 min bodyweight
5 rounds for time:
10 burpees
15 air squats
10 push-ups
20 hollow rocks
Rest 1 min between rounds
This hits well on days your body is beat up but you want to move. Scale push-ups to knees if chest/shoulders are smoked from gym days.
C3

Med Ball Complex

Conditioning
~20 min med ball + bodyweight
AMRAP 18 min:
10 med ball squat cleans
10 med ball Russian twists (each side)
10 med ball push-ups (hands on ball)
10 burpees
15 sit-ups
Med ball squat cleans are a sneaky-good clean substitute — full hip extension, catch in front-rack squat. Great for clean mechanics when overhead isn't an option.
C4

Death by Deadlift

Conditioning Strength
~25 min barbell
Every 2 min × 10 rounds:
3 deadlifts (building — add 10 lb every other round)
Max burpees with remaining time

Start ~60% of your 1RM. End heavy but not failing.
Score = total burpees across all rounds.
The 2-minute clock runs continuously. After your deadlifts, do burpees until you decide to stop — the remaining time in that window is your rest. Push burpees too long and you'll pay for it on the next set of deadlifts. That tradeoff is the whole game.
C5

21-15-9

Conditioning
~15–20 min barbell
For time — 21-15-9:
Deadlifts
Burpees

Pick a weight you can do the first 21 deadlifts in 2 sets max.
Simple and brutal. Rest as needed between movements but keep moving. Post your time and try to beat it next time you pull this session.
C6

Barbell Complex AMRAP

Conditioning AMRAP
~20 min barbell
AMRAP 15 min:
5 hang power cleans
5 front squats (from the clean, no re-rack)
5 barbell rows

One barbell, one weight the whole time. ~95–115 lb.
Score = rounds + reps.
The front squats coming directly out of the clean make this a real test of front rack endurance. If your wrists or elbows fatigue, that's useful information.
C7

KB + DB Chipper

Conditioning
~20–25 min KB + DB
For time:
30 KB swings — 53# KB
25 DB goblet squats — 35# DB
20 DB push press — 25# DBs
15 DB Romanian deadlifts — 35# DBs
10 burpees
20 DB goblet squats — 35# DB
30 KB swings — 53# KB
The palindrome structure means you finish on the thing that hurt at the start. Break up the swings however you need — the 53# will get heavy fast.
C8

Med Ball Tabata

Conditioning
~20 min med ball + bodyweight
Tabata (8 × 20 sec on / 10 sec off) per movement.
Rest 1 min between movements.

1. Med ball squat cleans
2. Burpees
3. Sit-ups
4. Push-ups

Score = lowest round of each movement.
Tabata scoring rewards consistency over sprinting. Try to hold the same rep count every round rather than blowing out early.
C9

KB Swing EMOM

Conditioning EMOM
~25 min KB + bodyweight
EMOM × 20 min — alternating:
Odd: 15 KB swings — 53# KB
Even: 10 burpees

Then: 2 min rest, then —
3 rounds:
10 KB goblet squats — 53# KB
10 push-ups
Rest 45 sec
Russian swings (hip height) are fine here — American swings (overhead) aren't worth the ceiling risk in your basement.

Skill & Accessory Sessions

SK1

Clean Technique Day

Skill
~30 min light barbell
Drill Sequence — run through 3×
  • 5 slow deadlifts to knee — pause, feel position
  • 5 high pulls from hang (feel the shrug + elbow drive)
  • 5 muscle cleans (no re-bend, just arms)
  • 5 hang power cleans — focus on punch under
  • 5 full hang squat cleans
Finisher: 5 × 3 hang power cleans @ 70%
1 complex per minute. Video yourself and check positions.
This is your "free practice" session — the stuff that gets rushed in class. No PRs, just reps with intent.
SK2

Handstand + Gymnastics Work

Skill Accessory
~25 min bodyweight
Handstand Practice — 10 min
  • Wall-facing handstand holds — 5 × max duration
  • Kick-up practice — 10 attempts, focus on stacked position
  • Pike push-ups if handstands aren't there yet — 4 × 8
Hang Bar Work — 10 min
  • Scapular pulls — 4 × 8 (retract/depress at top)
  • L-sit hold in hang — 5 × max (knees or straight legs)
  • Slow knee raises — 3 × 10 (controlled, no kipping)
Core Finisher
  • 3 × 30 sec hollow body hold
  • 3 × 10 tuck-ups
Scapular pulls while hanging are often more valuable than full pull-ups for building the shoulder stability needed for kipping and muscle-ups.
SK3

Active Recovery Flow

Recovery
~20 min bodyweight + med ball
2 rounds, easy pace, no rush:
World's greatest stretch × 5 each side
10 slow air squats (pause at bottom)
10 med ball good mornings
10 hip circles each direction
30 sec dead hang
10 push-ups (slow, perfect)
20 sec hollow body hold
Use this on days when everything feels beaten up. Moving beats not moving — this will leave you feeling better than before you started.
SK4

Pulling Mechanics

Skill Accessory
~25 min hang bar + barbell + DB
Pulling Progression
  • Scapular pulls — 4 × 8 on hang bar
  • Slow negative "pull-ups" — 4 × 6 (jump to top, lower 5–7 sec)
  • Barbell rows — 3 × 10, 2-second squeeze at top
  • DB single-arm rows — 3 × 10 each arm, 35# DB
Finisher
  • Accumulate 60 sec dead hang total (feet lifted)
Slow negatives build pulling strength fast when you can't do full pull-ups yet. The controlled descent recruits the same muscles as the pull-up itself.
SK5

Posterior Chain Accessory

Accessory Strength
~25 min barbell + KB
4 × 10 single-leg RDL — 35# KB in opposite hand
3 × 15 good mornings — light barbell, slow
3 × 10 reverse hyper sub: face-down on floor, lift both legs, hold 2 sec
3 × 12 KB swings — 53# KB
3 × 30 sec hollow body hold
This session will make your hamstrings and lower back very unhappy the next day in a good way. CrossFit consistently underloads this stuff.
SK6

Snatch Primer

Skill
~30 min empty / light barbell
Drill Sequence — 3 rounds
  • 5 snatch-grip deadlifts to knee — pause, feel position
  • 5 hang muscle snatches (no re-bend, punch overhead)
  • 5 hang power snatches — focus on transition under bar
  • 5 overhead squats with empty bar — or broomstick for shoulder mobility work if ceiling is too low
Keep this light — the goal is positions, not load. If the basement ceiling cuts off overhead squats, do them with a broomstick in a lower ceiling-safe depth to drill shoulder mobility instead.
SK7

Midline Specialist

Skill Accessory
~20 min med ball + hang bar
4 rounds:
30 sec hollow body hold
10 V-ups
30 sec arch hold
10 supermans
Rest 30 sec

Then — 3 rounds:
10 med ball Russian twists each side
10 med ball sit-ups
15 knee raises on hang bar
Quality over speed throughout. The hollow/arch pairing builds the body tension that makes kipping, toes-to-bar, and bar muscle-ups possible.
SK8

Pistol & Single-Leg Progressions

Skill
~25 min bodyweight + barbell + bench
Pistol Progressions — 15 min
  • Box pistols to bench — 4 × 5 each leg (sit back controlled)
  • Assisted pistols holding rack upright — 3 × 5 each leg
  • Freestanding attempts — 3 × max each leg, even just the descent counts
Supplemental — 3 rounds
  • 8 Bulgarian split squats each leg — rear foot on bench, barbell across back or hold KBs
  • 10 KB goblet squats — 53# KB, focus on depth
Bulgarian split squats with the bench are excellent and will expose any balance or mobility asymmetries between legs that bilateral squats hide.
SK9

KB Skill & Shoulder Health

Skill Accessory
~25 min KB + DB
KB Skill — 15 min
  • KB Turkish get-up — 5 × 1 each side, 35# KB (slow and deliberate, every position matters)
  • KB windmill — 3 × 5 each side, 35# KB
Shoulder Accessory — 2 rounds
  • 15 DB lateral raises — 10# DBs, slow
  • 15 DB front raises — 10# DBs
  • 15 DB rear delt flyes on bench — 10# DBs
  • 10 DB external rotation each arm — 10# DB
Turkish get-ups are one of the most complete movements you can do — shoulder stability, hip mobility, core control, and coordination all in one. The 35# KB is a solid working weight once you're comfortable with the movement.
SK10

Unilateral Strength Day

Accessory Strength
~30 min DB + KB + bench
4 × 8 each side — DB single-arm bench press, 35# DB
4 × 8 each side — single-arm DB row, 35# DB
3 × 10 each side — single-leg KB RDL, 35# KB
3 × 10 each side — KB single-arm press, 35# KB
3 × 12 — DB goblet squat, 35# DB
Unilateral work exposes and corrects the strength imbalances that bilateral lifts mask. If one side is notably weaker, start with it and match reps on the stronger side — don't compensate.
Suggested weekly mix (3 CF days): On your 4 off-days, try: 1 strength session, 1 conditioning session, 1 skill/accessory session, and 1 full rest day. If you're going to CF 2×/week still, you can do 2 strength + 1 conditioning or swap based on what CF hit that week. Avoid doubling up on posterior chain sessions (S1, S2, S5, SK5) back to back.
